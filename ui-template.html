A guide to your reference desk

Research with context

Find and compare

Search a name, abbreviation, or research interest. Filter by area, type, or evidence. Select Compare on two to four cards to inspect them together.

Go from overview to detail

Open a profile for its plain-English role, grouped amounts, research limits, mechanisms, approval context, cautions, and direct source links.

Add to your catalog

Use Add compound for a new entry, or Edit inside a profile to add strengths and research. Personal additions and edits are visibly marked.

Online and offline

Online edits save to your private catalog when connected. Offline copy downloads a complete editable HTML file. Offline changes stay on that browser until you export and import a data backup. Source links need internet access.

Read evidence labels precisely

Approved medicine existsAn ingredient has an approved medicinal product for a specific indication. This does not approve the screenshot product, every formulation, or other claimed uses.
Human researchHuman data exists, but study quality, size, outcomes, and relevance vary. See the profile for limits.
Early / preclinicalEvidence is mainly laboratory or animal research. Human benefit and safety may remain unestablished.
Evidence unclearThe formula, name, evidence base, or relevance cannot yet be established adequately.

Names, amounts, and product labels came from the six supplied screenshots. “Tested” is the list’s wording, not independent confirmation. Stock notes are historical and are not live availability. Amounts and volumes are preserved as listed, with missing units flagged. Different blends, derivatives, and formulations are not treated as interchangeable.

Carry your edits with you

Use an offline HTML copy to read the entire catalog without a connection. Export a JSON backup when you want to transfer edits between the offline file and the online catalog. Import previews additions and collisions before making changes. If online edits conflict, both versions are kept for review.
